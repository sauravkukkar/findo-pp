Privacy Policy for FinDo

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 | Last Updated: September 16, 2026

FinDo ("we", "our", or "us") provides a personal finance management, expense tracking, and subscription organizer mobile application (the "App"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, our data retention practices, and your rights in compliance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted credentials.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: User-entered expense/income amounts, categories, dates, account balances, budgets, and recurring subscriptions.
  • Receipt Images & OCR: When you utilize our receipt scanner, images captured via camera or gallery are processed using Google Gemini AI to extract merchant names, dates, and amounts.

B. Automatically Collected Data

  • Payment Reference Data: In-app subscriptions (FinDo Pro ₹199 / year) are securely processed via Razorpay. We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank PINs on our servers.
  • Advertising Identifiers: For free-tier users, Google AdMob may collect device advertising IDs to serve banner and interstitial advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to deliver core personal finance features, synchronize data across your authorized devices via Parse Server, verify subscription status, and display ads to free-tier users.

Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial records to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Camera: Used only when photographing physical receipts for expense capture.
  • Photo Library: Used only when selecting receipt photos from gallery storage.
  • Internet: Required to authenticate, sync financial data, and load advertisements.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver optional reminders for upcoming subscription bill renewals and budget thresholds.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard service providers who process data in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Gemini Vision AI (Receipt OCR)
  • Razorpay (Secure Payment Gateway)
  • Parse Server / Back4App (Encrypted Cloud Database)

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, you have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open FinDo → Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  • Permanent Purge: Upon confirmation, your user profile, transaction history, accounts, budgets, and subscriptions are immediately and permanently erased from our production databases.
  • Manual Request: You may also email support@findo.app from your registered address to request manual data wiping within 48 hours.

6. Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is secured using TLS/SSL encryption. Authentication tokens are saved in encrypted device storage.

7. Children's Privacy

FinDo is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
